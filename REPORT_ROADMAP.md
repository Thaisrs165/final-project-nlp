## 1. Hard rules from the course page (breaking these = F)

- **IEEE conference template.** Use the official LaTeX template, e.g. Overleaf's "IEEE Conference Template". No other template.
- **At most 2 pages, *including* figures, tables and references.** No abstract needed.
- **Required sections:** Introduction · Methods and datasets · Results · Conclusion · References.
- **Appendix:** optional and not graded. **The main text can't cite it.** Anything a claim depends on must be in the 2 pages.
- **Material:** deliver the code (this repo) and the app link.
- **Interview:** the professor may interview any of us, recorded, at a whiteboard. If one person can't explain a key point, the **whole group's grade can be capped** (F if you can't explain it, D if you only paraphrase). See section 5.

## 4. Decisions needed before writing (🛑)

1. **Objective targets from prior work.** The rubric requires a target; PLAN.md only says "report X with CIs". Proposed:
   - **Q1:** BERT's accuracy on canonical rows reaches the human baseline (from the agreement sheets) on real franchises, and the gap to the invented world is > 0 with a 95% CI that excludes 0 (LAMA-style probing, Petroni 2019).
   - **Q2:** if BERT reads traits, name_swap accuracy is not significantly lower than no_name (McNemar, p ≥ 0.05).
   - **Q3:** negation flip rate compared with Ettinger (2020), who found BERT largely insensitive to negation; on ambiguous items, BERT's JSD to the reference is lower than a uniform baseline's.

## 5. Interview prep: everyone must be able to explain these

- Why PLL-word-l2r and not a single `[MASK]` (group names are several word pieces; plain PLL inflates long names).
- What calibration does, and why the named prior uses "N/A" for the name.
- Why an invented world (a control that rules out memorization).
- Why occlusion and not attention weights (Jain & Wallace 2019).
- Cohen's κ, JSD, McNemar and bootstrap CI: what each one measures, and in one sentence.
- Why the panel is simulated, and why that's a limitation.
- How traits were picked, and how leakage was prevented.
- "Explain the project in 30 seconds."
- "How does MLM compare with NLI?"
