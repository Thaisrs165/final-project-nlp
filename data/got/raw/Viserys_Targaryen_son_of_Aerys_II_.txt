Personality
Viserys has a tempestuous temper. He is petty, demanding, arrogant, and self-aggrandizing. He lacks patience and resents when he has to set aside his pride or when he is made to feel powerless. When made to feel powerless or forced to accept situations he does not like at the expense of his pride, he turns to take this out on someone he sees as even more powerless, or under his power, such as his sister or a servant, in order to feel in control and powerful again.
His attitude and behavior make him dislikable and many find him annoying and childish. Viserys is given to posturing, saying that he is a "dragon" and upsetting him will "wake the dragon", and also posturing that he is a rightful monarch and owed deference as a matter of course.
Viserys is not unintelligent. He purchased Doreah for his sister to assist her in winning Khal Drogo over, and this turned out to be vital to the success of their marriage. He is able to recognize that the likelihood of Khal Drogo ever giving him the promised army is slim, as is his chance of taking back the Iron Throne for himself, without Khal Drogo and Daenerys stepping in instead. He knows that, as a man, he is expected to take back his thrown himself, while Daenerys is able to attach herself to a powerful, established ruler through marriage and use that as a stepping stone not available to him. However, his inability to resolve these issues, or see a way to be respected and followed, cause him to lash out in destructive ways from frustration, shame, and jealousy.
Viserys is a very young man who is very angry at the world and feels owed.
His personality stems from the duality of his situation.
On the one hand, he is a prince who has steadfastly worked towards his goal of a Targaryen restoration, instead of giving up and moving on to a normal life. He has been told from childhood that he is owed Westeros and a crown and fed dreams of grandeur. He survived, and ensured his sister survived, under desperation conditions and avoiding assassins. He was able to secure supporters, though he had little to offer. He gained the promise of an army. He has been able to claw himself and his sister out of the gutter, against monumental challenges. This is where he gets his arrogance, self-assurance, and conviction that he will one day regain his throne.
But it is also true that he has never received a proper education for a ruler. He has no sword training, or knowledge of state craft, management, or diplomacy. He was never taught how to lead others. He survived and ensured his sister's survival by being servile, and often had to beg, which left deep scars of resentment. He has very little to offer a powerful ally, and the reality is that anyone offering to help put him on the throne would be doing so for their own gain, and because it served them to have a puppet king in their debt. This turns his conviction into a delusion. He is careful to protect himself from anything which threatens his self-image, often leading to his petty, childish, and demanding behaviors.
