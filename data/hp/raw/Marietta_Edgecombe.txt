Personality and traits
— Cho Chang defending Marietta
When Cho became depressed over Cedric's death, Marietta was presumably comforting and supportive, being the only one to remain with her when the other friends left. Cho claimed Marietta was a lovely person, an attribute that was mostly unseen in her behaviour to others.
However, Marietta was one of the many people who were loyal to the Ministry of Magic and was sceptical of Harry Potter's statement of Lord Voldemort returning. She thought that he and Albus Dumbledore were liars and remained cynical towards him throughout her time in Dumbledore's Army, and would not be swayed by the genuine article within The Quibbler about Voldemort's return; she only joined because Cho wanted her to. As with the majority of the magical community, Marietta feared to hear the name "Voldemort", as she shrieked aloud and slopped Butterbeer down her front.
Marietta's mother was a Ministry employee and as such, Marietta felt strong pressure to be on Dolores Umbridge's good side. Her apparent faithfulness to Cho diminished when Cho forced her to join an illegal organisation, and she eventually betrayed the D.A., meaning her classmates (including Cho) who were in the organisation were almost expelled from school. Even if it meant that she would forsake a chance to learn more practical defence, especially the Patronus Charm that others were so eager to learn, Marietta chose her family's loyalty first. While Cho insisted that Marietta simply made a mistake, Harry felt that Marietta was disgraceful and an unforgivable traitor. Hermione Granger also commented that she was "stupid".1]
When her face was horribly disfigured for her betrayal against the D.A., she became extremely embarrassed and refused to uncover her face to testify verbally, leading to some setbacks for Umbridge and Fudge that ultimately gave Kingsley a chance to modify her memory. Because of the jinx scarring her face, she took great measures to cover her disfigured appearance, and even became anxious in Harry's presence afterwards, implying she may have felt some guilt over her treacherous behaviour, but lacked the courage to apologise to him.
She was shown to be a poor judge of character, as she did not believe Harry and Dumbledore's story, instead believing the Ministry's smear campaign against them. She was also very critical and insolent towards Harry during their D.A. meetings, implying she was rather impolite and unpleasant in nature, at least to people she distrusted.
