Personality and traits
— Description of the Friar's cheerful deposition
The Friar was very cheerful and welcoming, and happily greeted new students at Hogwarts.1] He also tended to find something to value in anyone and look past their faults, as he always insisted with the other ghosts to let Peeves attend the Start-of-Term Feasts despite his many wrongdoings.1]2] He also appeared to be quite sociable, meeting and engaging in conversation with fellow ghosts during Nicholas de Mimsy-Porpington's five-hundredth Deathday Party.3] He also seems to have some House pride, as he happily reminisced about his "old House" when welcoming first-years.1] Despite this, he was always resentful of the fact that he was never made a cardinal.4] His overly kindness has proven to be his undoing, as he would heal the pox simply by poking with a stick, and pull rabbits out of the communion cup, showing recklessness in terms of concealing his wizardry.
