Personality and traits
— Hermione Granger regarding Myrtle's over-emotional nature
Myrtle was constantly bullied at Hogwarts, for both her physical appearance and her personality. Myrtle's personality itself was being very sensitive; she hardly ever smiled and took great offence at the smallest slight. She usually cried when she got sad, and when she did, she cried very loudly, hence her nickname Moaning Myrtle.
She was often upset so badly that she tried to kill herself until she realised that she was already dead.1] Myrtle did tend to be happy when something bad happened to other students, like when Hermione Granger accidentally gained cat fur, ears and a tail after taking a contaminated Polyjuice Potion.2] The only time she seemed to enjoy herself was when she recalled the moment of death, describing the event with relish.3]
— Myrtle's self-conscious personality
She did, however, seem to warm to some male students, including Harry Potter and Draco Malfoy.4] Myrtle also appeared to be a desperate flirt; she constantly flirted with Harry, and showed her desperation to find love by spying on students such as Cedric Diggory whilst he bathed in the prefects' bathroom.5] Myrtle was likely childish as well, as she liked to take bubble baths when she was alive, due to 'all those pretty bubbles'.6]
