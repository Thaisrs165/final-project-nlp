What house was Hannah Abbott sorted into when she started attending Hogwarts?

Who taught and led the organisation Hannah Abbott was a part of in her fifth year at Hogwarts?
Hannah Abbott was a part of Dumbledore's Army in her fifth year at Hogwarts, an organisation that was taught and led by the famous Harry Potter.

Why was Hannah Abbott pulled out of school in 1996?
In 1996, Hannah Abbott was pulled out of school because she received the tragic news that her mother had been found dead. This event deeply affected Hannah and had a significant impact on her life at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

Personality and traits
Hannah appeared to have a naive streak since she was easily convinced by Ernie that Harry might be the Heir of Slytherin, despite having always considered him to be nice.1] She also believed that Sirius Black could turn into a flowering shrub (although, given that he was, in fact, an Animagus, the belief wasn't too far from the truth).2] Hannah was also willing to ostracise and harass Harry due to her suspecting him of trying to steal Cedric's glory in the Triwizard Tournament, displaying a negative side to her house loyalty.
Hannah was easily flustered and appeared to lack self-confidence, as was evidenced by her breakdown in Herbology and her claims of being stupid.3] It seemed that, when under pressure, she was prone to making mistakes.4] Despite this, Hannah was a loyal friend and showed her bravery firstly by joining Dumbledore's Army,5] and then fighting in the defence of Hogwarts.6]
