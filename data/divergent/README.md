## Status (2026-11-18)
- `characters.csv` is the **worksheet**: 26 characters with the faction verified on Wikipedia (quote in `faction_evidence`). The `traits` and `trait_evidence` cells are empty, because the Divergent Fandom wiki blocks automated access (HTTP 402) and Wikipedia has almost no personality adjectives (the few it has are in `adjectives_seen_on_wikipedia`).
- **To do by hand:** open each character's Fandom page in the browser, copy 5 adjectives from the "Personality" section into `traits` (separated by `;`) and the sentence(s) into `trait_evidence`, and change `status` to `ok`. Five rows are `needs_faction_check` (Al, Lynn, Molly, Drew, Myra): confirm the faction first.
- `tests.csv` is **generated**, don't edit it by hand: `.venv/bin/python experiments/divergent.py build`. It copies the finished characters as `canonical` rows (it refuses rows with ≠ 5 traits, empty evidence, or a label leak) and adds the hand-designed probes: `clear` (11), `conflicting` (40), `divergent_mode` (20), `generic` (3). Rows of other test types (step 4) are kept untouched.
- Experiment and analysis: `experiments/divergent.py run` and `analyze`. Output in `results/divergent/` (`SUMMARY.md`, `t*.csv`, two figures).
- Class balance: 15 of 26 characters are Dauntless; Amity has 2 and Candor 1. Report macro-F1 and the majority baseline, not only accuracy.
