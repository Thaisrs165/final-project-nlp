Quick Answers
Who was the Avatar immediately before and after Roku?
The Avatar immediately before Roku was Avatar Kyoshi, and the Avatar immediately after Roku was Avatar Aang.
Provided by: Fandom
What role did Roku's memory play in Aang's quest to end the Hundred Year War?
Roku's memory served as a guiding force for Aang throughout his quest to end the Hundred Year War. Roku, being the Avatar before Aang, had experienced the onset of the war and his memories provided Aang with valuable insights and lessons. For instance, Roku warned Aang about the importance of being decisive, reflecting on his own regret of sparing Fire Lord Sozin's life, which ultimately led to the war and Roku's death. This advice significantly influenced Aang's approach towards ending the war.
Provided by: Fandom
How did Avatar Roku die?
Avatar Roku met his end during a volcanic eruption on his home island. Despite his mastery of the Avatar State and lavabending, he was unable to save himself from the disaster. In his final moments, Roku's dragon, Fang, curled up with him, and they died together.
Provided by: Fandom
Who was Avatar Roku's wife?
Avatar Roku's wife was Ta Min, a noblewoman from the Fire Nation.
Provided by: Community
Is Zuko related to Roku, and if so, how?
Yes, Zuko is indeed related to Roku. Zuko is the great-grandson of Avatar Roku. This connection comes from Zuko's mother's side of the family, making Zuko a direct descendant of Roku. This familial link also means that Zuko is spiritually connected to Aang, who is the reincarnation of Avatar Roku.

His affectionate nature remained even after his death as he offered his successor, Aang, a warm hug despite the young airbender damaging his connection to Roku and his other past lives.2]
Even though he had a noble upbringing, his journey around the world to master the four elements made him realize what his privilege had afforded him, and he understood what it was like for those who lived in poverty. These experiences allowed him to understand his duty as the Avatar.3] He appreciated novelty and individuality. Traveling extensively, he knew that the world was full of beautiful unique things. He believed that people were stronger when their talents were valued, and took time to listen to what others had to say.4]
Roku believed that friendship could transcend a single lifetime and showed immense devotion to his friends and loved ones. When recalling his youth with Prince Sozin, he did so with fondness and without any bitterness about how things later soured between them because of Sozin's ambition. However, this devotion also proved to be a weakness because upon having a chance to stop Sozin's mad conquest later in life, Roku spared him in honor of their friendship.1] His traumatic death and the war that followed caused Roku's spirit to regret sparing his old friend and he advised Aang against making the same mistake, telling him that it would be best to kill Fire Lord Ozai.5] This was also shown when Aang was in a predicament about whether to kill Zuko as per the promise he made to him if he ever acted like his father. After Aang refused to do so, Roku criticized him for his indecision.6]
Roku was a wise and experienced man, and exuded competence and skill to others after he became a fully-realized Avatar.4] Maintaining the peace between the Four Nations, Roku did everything in his power to negotiate, calm, and guide potentially volatile situations.4] If necessary, he was ready to use the threat of violence to enforce his decisions.1]7] However, Roku was also aware of the limits of a single man's perception, telling Aang that he did not have all the answers and could only advise him from his own point of view.2] He was self-critical, believing himself to be an indecisive man and acknowledging that Sozin had used his own merciful nature against him as Roku could have prevented the Hundred Year War if he had been decisive enough to kill the Fire Lord.5] Roku was not above intimidating people in order to maintain balance in the world but rarely followed through with his threats, something he regretted after his death.1]8]
