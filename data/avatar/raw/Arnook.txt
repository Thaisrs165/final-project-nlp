How did Arnook become the chief of the Northern Water Tribe?
Arnook assumed the role of the Northern Water Tribe chief after his father's demise, over two decades before Team Avatar's North Pole visit. His leadership, despite not being a waterbender, earned him respect. He married a tribal chieftain's daughter and had a daughter, Princess Yue.

How did Arnook save his daughter's life?
Arnook's daughter, Yue, was born gravely ill. In a desperate attempt to save her life, he took his daughter to the Spirit Oasis and the Moon and Ocean spirits dwelt in their mortal forms. Placing Yue in the waters of the oasis during a full moon, she was revitalized through part of the Moon Spirit's own life force.
Provided by: Community
Despite not being a waterbender, how did Arnook earn respect from his tribe?
Arnook, the Northern Water Tribe chief, earned respect from his tribe through his noble and brave leadership. He prioritized his citizens' concerns, even though he wasn't a waterbender.
