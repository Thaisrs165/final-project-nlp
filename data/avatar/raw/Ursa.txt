Quick Answers
How many children did Ursa have?
Ursa was mother to three children: with Ozai she had two children, Zuko and Azula, and after her banishment she also had daughter, Kiyi, with Noren.
Provided by: Community
What is the prophecy that led Ursa to marry Ozai?
A prophecy made by the Fire Sages foretold that a powerful lineage of rulers would come forth from a marital union between a descendant of Avatar Roku and the Fire Lord's bloodline. Ursa, as a descendant of Avatar Roku, was therefore chosen to marry Ozai by Fire Lord Azulon, despite her previous relationship with Ikem.
Provided by: Community
What happened to Ursa after she was banished?
After her banishment, Ursa returned to her hometown of Hira'a where she met her childhood love Ikem, now with a new face and going by the name Noren. Noren took her to the Mother of Faces, who gave her a new identity and removed her memories of her previous life. Ursa, now going by the name Noriko, and Noren would then get married, and later have a daughter, Kiyi.
Provided by: Community
What sort of person was Ursa?
Ursa is a character defined by her strong motherly love and protectiveness. She strove to be a kind, caring, and loving mother, showing equal authority in parenting as her much more powerful husband. She was particularly close to Zuko, encouraging his determination and resorting to any lengths to protect him, including being involved in the murder of Fire Lord Azulon. Her relationship with Azula was much more strained and complex, being forced to discipline her daughter on a regular basis. It was Ursa's behaviour towards Azula that started the princess' anger towards her brother, and also led Azula to believe her mother considered her a monster. Ursa, however, cared about her daughter's well-being, and later came to regret the pain her actions had caused her.
Provided by: Community
What was Ursa's role in the death of Azulon?
After hearing of Azulon's order for Ozai to kill Zuko, an order Ozai had every intention of carrying out, Ursa made a deal with her husband to spare Zuko's life. She provided him with an untraceable poison to kill Azulon, after which he could then become Fire Lord. Ozai agreed, on the condition that she leave the city and never return.
Provided by: Community
As she knew that her marriage to someone with the personality and rank as Ozai would mean she would be constantly isolated and seen by vigilantes, she was very careful and vigilant herself, knowing the servants who she could not trust and hid her medical skills from her then-husband. However, Ursa's forceful attitude was shown even against his fury when she confirmed that he read her letters through a risky plan, which would indirectly affect Zuko negatively at the end.2] Ultimately, Ozai's emotional abusiveness toward her and his children resulted in Ursa developing a lasting fear of him,3] connected to her deep worries about her family's safety.4]5] She also developed a considerable dislike for elements of the Fire Nation's imperialist system, notably its classicist and strength-focused education system.5]
Ursa was rather artistic and perseverant, She always wanted to get the part as the Dragon Empress in Love amongst the Dragons. Her love for the play extended even to her isolated adulthood, showing it to her children who acted as the main rivals in the play. Ursa was a pragmatist, choosing to leave Ikem behind in order to protect him and later accepted banishment in order to save Zuko. Despite her love and affection for her children, she opted to forget their existence at one point in order to find inner peace once she reunited with Noren. Ultimately, she came to regret this decision, realizing that Zuko and Azula were her true family.2]
Ursa was a kind, caring, and loving mother to her first two children, showing equal authority in their parenting as her much more powerful then-husband. One of her defining features was her strong motherly love and protectiveness of her only son Zuko, whom she encouraged to be as he is, especially his determination.6] Her life was dedicated to safeguarding him from any harm, resorting to any means necessary to keep her son safe such as to give Ozai a vial of poison, aware that he would use it to kill Fire Lord Azulon and take the throne.7] Similarly, Ursa's relationship with her youngest daughter, Kiyi, was very loving and caring despite it temporary got strained after obtaining her original face. She was overprotective as well, as she panicked when she saw Kiyi riding a flying dolphin fish, being fearful of losing her second daughter too.3] Together with the traumatic memories of her first marriage, these worries contributed to Ursa's intense fear of losing her children in some way permanently. She only became better at dealing with this fear after Iroh told her that it would never disappear but could be better dealt with them by seeing it with unclouded eyes.4] She subsequently strove to avoid mistakes she had made with Azula, hoping that Kiyi could grow up in a better environment.5]
However, Ursa's relationship with her eldest daughter Azula was strained by the fact that the young princess was emotionally abusive and manipulative toward her family and friends, especially Zuko. She was forced to discipline Azula on a regular basis, but it was apparent to her that her daughter's cruelty was beyond a normal level.8] It was her behavior toward Azula that started the princess' anger and jealousy toward Zuko for how he was loved and cared for by her as she herself craved their mother's love. Although her treatment was good-intentioned, the way she disciplined Azula regularly made her daughter incorrectly believe that Ursa thought of her as a monster;9] her belief that Ursa never loved her influenced Azula's later behavior and emotional instability.10] However, despite all this, Ursa cared about her daughter's well-being, as she wondered, teary-eyed, if Azula was safe, warm, or happy after learning that she had vanished in the Forgetful Valley and appeared to regret how her attempts to make Azula a better person had caused her daughter such pain.3]
