What is the relationship between Jinora and Kai?
Jinora and Kai, often referred to as 'Kainora' or 'Jinkai' by fans, share a strong bond that began when they first met in a small village in the Earth Kingdom. Their relationship strengthened during Kai's airbending training, and they spent much time together, even going on adventures such as looking for young sky bison. Jinora expressed romantic feelings for Kai early on, often blushing in his presence and offering to teach him airbending moves. They were even captured together by bison rustlers, but were freed with the help of other airbending students and Tenzin. By 174 AG, Jinora and Kai had started dating.

How did Jinora become the youngest known airbending master?
Jinora, the eldest daughter of Tenzin, showed an early interest in books and was generally quiet, which allowed her to focus on her airbending training. She was naturally gifted and had a unique affinity with spirits, which was a rare ability among humans. This connection to the spiritual world played a significant role in her mastery of airbending. As a result, she became the youngest known airbending master, surpassing even Avatar Aang's record. She was also the first of her generation to achieve this status.
Provided by: Fandom
What is the significance of Jinora's tattoos?
Jinora's tattoos are a significant symbol of her mastery over airbending. These arrow tattoos, which run down an Air Nomad's chi paths, are an emulation of the natural arrows on the heads of flying bisons, the original airbenders revered by the Air Nomads. Jinora received her tattoos at the age of eleven, marking her as a master airbender. This is a tradition among the Air Nomads, where an airbender must pass thirty-six levels of airbending, or create a new airbending technique, to earn their tattoos.
Provided by: Fandom
Who are Jinora's siblings?
Jinora, the daughter of Tenzin, has three siblings. She has a younger sister named Ikki and two younger brothers named Meelo and Rohan. They all reside on Air Temple Island, near Republic City.
Provided by: Community
What is Jinora's role in the Air Nation?
Jinora is the firstborn child and elder daughter of Tenzin and Pema. She lives on Air Temple Island with her parents and her three younger siblings: Ikki, Meelo, and Rohan. Jinora is known for her serious and mellow nature, contrasting with her more hyper and playful siblings. She also holds a significant role as the first female airbending master in 171 years and the second airbending master since the Air Nomad Genocide.

Jinora was very inquisitive and highly driven by thirst for knowledge. As such, she usually spent much of her time reading books and educating herself on history, science, folklore, and spirituality. Her inquisitive nature also drove her to understand other people, as she showed interest in Korra's love life. Her accumulation of vast knowledge was the source of her confidence, which allowed her to feel more prepared in the face of anxious situations, such as when she encountered Wan Shi Tong, whom Jinora was able to outwit by using her knowledge of history and technology, and taking advantage of the spirit's isolation from the material world since the Hundred Year War. In the same encounter, Jinora displayed pride and a somewhat conceited attitude, although not vain, when lecturing Wan Shi Tong. This attitude led her sister Ikki to perceive her as "above it all".3]
Jinora also displayed a great amount of courage, as she was willing to exchange her soul in favor of the world's well-being when she was being used as leverage to coerce Korra into opening the Northern spirit portal,3] as well as prolong the separation of her soul and body in order to aid the world by finding Raava.4] As Jinora grew older, in no small part due to Kai's influence on her, she began to show an assertive side to her when standing up for herself and her beliefs, sometimes even to the point of being rebellious. She argued with Tenzin when he stated that she was too young and inexperienced to receive her airbending tattoos,5] and also refused to leave the Republic City spirit portal after Wonyong Keum planned to exploit it.6]
Although an intellectual, Jinora was much more interested in abstract and theoretical knowledge. This natural detachment from the Physical World has increased the girl's spiritual awareness and intuitive thinking, which allowed her to surpass Tenzin, whose mindset was more empirical and literal than his daughter's, to become the spiritual master he always aspired to be. Jinora's intuitive mindset allowed her to quickly grasp the nature of the spirits, strengthening her affinity to them and their respect for her. She was initially concerned about her father's reaction to it upon learning of Tenzin's insecurity about his own lack of spiritual affinity, fearing he was angry with her.7] She quickly intuited that the spirits were being affected by Korra's emotions when guiding her in the Spirit World.3]
After Harmonic Convergence created new airbenders, Jinora began to take a leadership role, assisting Tenzin in training them,5] and even coordinating them against Zaheer.8] These experiences became very important to her sense of self and her identity as a spiritual expert, so much so that not being taken seriously by some of them despite her efforts interfered with her ability to meditate into the Spirit World and commune with spirits for a while, until Asami and Korra both shared how their similar struggles led to a better understanding of themselves.9]
