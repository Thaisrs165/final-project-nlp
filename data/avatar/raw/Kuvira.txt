What is the significance of Kuvira's title 'The Great Uniter'?
Kuvira's title 'The Great Uniter' signifies her role in reuniting the fragmented Earth Kingdom. Over a period of three years, she managed to stabilize the Earth Kingdom and unite many of its states under her own banner. She offered protection to the nation's states from bandits in exchange for their loyalty, which earned her this title. However, her methods were often seen as forceful and authoritarian, leading some to view her more as a conqueror than a uniter.

How did Kuvira rise to power in the Earth Kingdom?
Kuvira, also known as the Great Uniter, rose to power in the Earth Kingdom through a series of strategic moves. After the Earth Kingdom fell into disarray, Kuvira successfully brought the capital city, Ba Sing Se, under her control. With the blessing of world leaders, she took command of the remaining Earth Kingdom military and set about restoring order to the nation. Over a period of three years, she managed to stabilize the Earth Kingdom and unite many of its states under her banner. Despite initially agreeing to transfer power back to the Earth Monarchy once stability was restored, Kuvira refused to relinquish power due to her lack of faith in the monarchy as a system of government. Instead, she dissolved the Earth Kingdom monarchy and created the Earth Empire with herself as its leader.
Provided by: Fandom
What role did Kuvira play in the Metal Clan before her rise to power?
Before her rise to power, Kuvira was a prominent member of the Metal Clan. She was adopted by Suyin Beifong and served as the captain of the guard in Zaofu. In addition to her military duties, Kuvira was also part of the city's dance troupe. Her leadership skills and mastery of metalbending made her a key figure within the clan.
Provided by: Fandom
What happened to Kuvira after she was defeated?
Three months after her failed invasion of Republic City, Kuvira was made to stand trial for her crimes. Facing the tribunal, she apologized for her actions but argued that she has restored order to the Earth Kingdom. During the Gaoling Crisis, Kuvira was temporarily released into Avatar Korra's custody and helped to bring about the permanent end of the Earth Empire she had created. In light of her remorse, she was sentenced to house arrest in Zaofu.
Provided by: Community
What is the relationship between Kuvira and Suyin Beifong?
Kuvira and Suyin Beifong share a complex relationship. Suyin took Kuvira in and supported her during her difficult times, acting as a mother figure. Kuvira, in turn, served as the captain of Suyin's guard and helped her fight against the Red Lotus. However, their relationship was strained due to Kuvira's actions, leading to a period of estrangement. Eventually, they reconciled, with Suyin assuring Kuvira that she will always be a member of the Beifong family.

Traumatized from her parents abandoning her as a child,2] Kuvira was driven by a fierce desire to protect and guide the citizens of the Earth Kingdom by achieving national unity through the use of military force. Described by Suyin Beifong as a complicated person, her willingness to usher in an age of solidarity and collective well-being was further nurtured by Suyin's progressive ideology, to which she was exposed from early childhood.3]
During her time in Zaofu, Kuvira was generally a caring person with a warm disposition, loyal and obedient to authority, following orders from her former superiors with no outward protest, even when she wanted to act beyond the confines of her position as the city's head of security.4]5] However, as the anarchy instigated by the Red Lotus escalated, Kuvira found herself increasingly at odds with Suyin, as her desire to reinforce the Earth Kingdom's security and prosperity led her to break away from the Metal Clan, labeling Suyin's refusal to do so as gutless.6] Leading the campaign to restore order to the Earth Kingdom, her demeanor changed to one of self-righteousness and hostility to those who defied or criticize her motives, having threatened to leave a group of bandits to die on a train rail if they to declined Kuvira's offer to serve her, in addition to having threatened Varrick's life when he refused to continue his spirit vine experiments. Moreover, Kuvira conveyed no hesitation in departing from the capital of Yi without providing aid to its impoverished citizens when the state's governor refused to accept her extortionate deal, while also berating him for failing to put his citizens' needs ahead of his own pride. While Kuvira was highly persuasive and capable of convincing state leaders to pledge their allegiance to her, she was proficient in intimidating individuals that refuse to comply with her requests.7]
Ultimately, Kuvira reached the conclusion that giving control of the Earth Kingdom to someone based on lineage was archaic, believing that she was best suited to govern the nation. Her relentlessness in reuniting the Earth Kingdom became a subsuming goal justifying all the means deemed necessary to achieve her vision, which included forcing citizens into slave labor, removing dissenters and people not of Earth Kingdom descent to reeducation camps, and experimented with weapons of mass destruction e.g. spirit vine technology.6] Kuvira's confidence in her own right and skill reached the point where she challenged Korra to a duel in order to prevent an all-out battle and possibly risk the lives of her soldiers, displaying both her personal bravery and a certain loyalty toward her army.8] Though she usually possessed a calm and serious composure, she could be short-tempered, agitated, and hostile when provoked or threatened, such as when she was heavily criticized by the governor of Yi7] and by her former subordinate Guan,9] or when the Colossus was being breached by the Satos.10]
Despite this, Kuvira also showed a softer side to her personality in her private life, urging Opal to reconcile with her family and gently easing Bolin's fears about her intentions. She was welcoming to her allies and fans,1] and demonstrated forgiveness to those loyal to her.6] Additionally, due to the more tolerant environment she was raised within in Zaofu, Kuvira notably lacked the prejudice towards non-heterosexual relationships that was historically prevalent in much of the Earth Kingdom, despite her regime’s extremely jingoistic ideology.9]
However, Kuvira was also willing to sacrifice anyone to achieve her goals, evident from when she decided to blow up the factory where her now ex-fiancé was kept to seize the opportunity of eliminating Avatar Korra.11] Nevertheless, she believed that any and all of her actions are ultimately aimed at improving the lives of the Earth Kingdom's citizens and was therefore willing to sacrifice individuals and even entire groups, but not at the greater cost of the majority. Suyin also stated that Kuvira strongly believed that her achievements outweighed her mistakes, resulting in a fatal lack of self-reflection. Kuvira herself had repeatedly stated that she regretted some of her actions, but ultimately regarded the Earth Empire as her foremost project to improve the lives of the people of the Earth Kingdom. Accordingly, she remained loyal to the Earth Empire's authoritarian ideology even after her defeat and showed no respect or sympathy for democracy.9] After seeing how angry her ex-fiancé was at her, she slowly began to understand that she made a huge mistake attempting to sacrifice him, as she completely destroyed any future of them being together. Before she departed, Kuvira gave a heartfelt apology to Baatar Jr. and told him that her love for him was completely genuine. It was only after Guan was defeated did Kuvira truly understand the harm she caused and in recognizing that she needed to accept responsibility for her dire actions, Kuvira willingly pleaded guilty during her trial. After Kuvira was placed in Suyin's custody, she was moved by how forgiving her adoptive mother was along with the rest of the Beifongs, who accepted Kuvira back into the family despite her actions.12]
