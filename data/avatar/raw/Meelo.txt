Who are Meelo's parents and what is their significance in the Avatar universe?
Tenzin and Pema are the parents of Meelo. Tenzin, the youngest son of Avatar Aang and Katara, grew up as the only other airbender in the world besides his father. Being the only airbending master after Aang's passing, he became Korra's airbending teacher. Tenzin also played an important political role in Republic City and in the continuation of the Air Nation and survival of airbending techniques. Pema, although not an airbender herself, is a central figure in the family, offering emotional support. She was also the mother of 3 confirmed airbenders, making her crucial to the survival of the airbending art.
Provided by: Community
What skills besides airbending does Meelo demonstrate?
Despite his often childish demeanour, Meelo is a fast learner, quickly mastering training of the lemurs on the island and later directing them in a coordinated display. He is also a gifted artist, creating a lifelike drawing of Korra after she went missing and painting a highly detailed self-portrait of himself.
Provided by: Community
How does Meelo's character evolve as he grows up?
As a young child, Meelo was very energetic and often disrupted the calmness of Air Temple Island. He was very goofy, much like his grandfather Avatar Aang, and prone to rambunctious outbursts and various comical comments. As he grew older, he developed a more serious and sometimes self-centered side, frequently ordering his sisters around and taking charge of the mission to locate Korra.
Provided by: Community
He also demonstrated a sassy side and a rather aggressive side as well, relishing his role in training the new airbender recruits in an aggressive and playfully frightening manner. During the training, he seemed to be act as a somewhat draconian commander or stereotypical boot camp officer.2]3] This trait developed more as he grew older, as he frequently ordered his sisters around.4] He also displayed a self-centered nature, as evidenced when he constantly viewed the mission to locate Korra and bring her home as his mission, whereas it was something he was doing with his sisters.5]
