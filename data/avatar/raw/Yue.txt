Personality
Yue was a remarkably kind, sweet, and gentle girl with a sense of humor. She offered hope to others even in crucial times, including a despaired Aang. She was courageous enough to sacrifice her own life to save the Moon Spirit, a feat that not only saved her tribe but the world.2]3]
Yue had a deep sense of loyalty and duty to her tribe. This is evident through her willingness to enter an arranged marriage with Hahn despite her affections for Sokka,2] and her choice to sacrifice her life to bring the Moon Spirit back. Her sense of duty and her spiritual nature were influenced by her upbringing in the Northern Water Tribe and the circumstances of her birth and ill-health as a child.3]4]2]
As the Moon Spirit, Yue initially retained much of her old personality, remaining playful, contemplative, and merciful. She was humble for a great spirit; when interacting with mortals at one point after her ascension, she asked them to just call her Yue due to not being used to the "whole worship-thing".1]
