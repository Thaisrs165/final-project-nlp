Quick Answers
How many children does Tenzin have?
Tenzin has four children with his wife Pema: two daughters, Jinora and Ikki, and two sons, Meelo and Rohan.
Provided by: Community
How old is Tenzin in "The Legend of Korra"?
Tenzin is 51 years old at the beginning of "The Legend of Korra", and the youngest of Avatar Aang's three children.
Provided by: Community
How strong is Tenzin at airbending?
As the only one of Avatar Aang's children to be born with airbending abilities, Tenzin was trained extensively by his father in all aspects of airbending, and after Aang's passing was the only airbending master in the world. In combat, Tenzin is strong enough to create air blasts powerful enough to knock back mecha tanks, and successfully fought one-on-one with Zaheer, himself a proficient airbender.
Provided by: Community
Where does Tenzin live?
Tenzin lives with his family on Air Temple Island, a small isle off the coast of Republic City in Yue Bay. Constructed by Avatar Aang, it is also home to the Air Acolytes, a group dedicated to preserving the culture and traditions of the Air Nomads, and colonies of flying bison and ring-tailed winged lemurs.
Provided by: Community
What is the significance of the Harmonic Convergence of 171 AG in Tenzin's life?
The Harmonic Convergence of 171 AG was a pivotal event in Tenzin's life. Following this event, many individuals across the Earth Kingdom suddenly developed the ability to airbend. As the son of Aang, the last airbender, Tenzin took it upon himself to train these new airbenders, helping to restore the Air Nation. He became the figurehead of the new Air Nation, representing it in global affairs. However, this also led to conflicts, such as when he helped airbenders escape from Earth Queen Hou-Ting's custody, leading to tensions with the Earth Kingdom.

He embodied the Air Nomad nature of being peaceful and calm, but could sometimes be riled up around his children or Korra due to their differing perspectives.1] Despite his generally calm demeanor, he could become aggressive and prone to do "whatever it takes", when his family was threatened or in any danger, exemplified by his willingness to use brute force to break through the Northern defenses surrounding the Southern spirit portal in order to save Jinora.3] This desperation to protect his family and nation instilled in him a dangerous inner fury that unleashed when his loved ones were threatened, much like his father.
Tenzin was a staunch traditionalist and, at times, could come across as rather inflexible and stubborn. This showed through his disdain for pro-bending, which he considered violent, irrelevant, and a perversion to more traditional forms of bending. However, after Korra joined the Fire Ferrets, he brushed up on the rules of the game and seemed better versed in the sport. In doing so, he became less antagonistic to it than before and even started cheering during one match. By doing this, he showed that he was willing to show a liking in the interests of those close to him.4]
Tenzin also appeared to be rather prideful and overbearing, as he hid his inability to enter the Spirit World from others, instead making up excuses that the people around him prevented him from doing so. When he found out that Jinora could see spirits, he grew jealous of her and kept stubbornly insisting that he should be the one to bring Korra to the Spirit World as Jinora would be unable to face its dangers. Following his sister's persuasion and daughter's assurance, he eventually relented and expressed his pride in Jinora.5]
Despite this, Tenzin still remained doubtful and unsure of himself, taking on a heavy burden in his self-imposed drive to live up to his father's legacy, which only served to impair his well-being and mentality. However, upon rescuing Jinora from the Fog of Lost Souls, Tenzin was able to overcome these insecurities, no longer seeing himself as a reflection of his father, and grew in both spirituality as well as self-confidence.6]
Tenzin would have done everything to preserve the new Air Nation, even dueling with all the Red Lotus' elemental masters. Even when he was bruised and battered by the Red Lotus' attack, he refused to give in, showing a strong resolve to protect his loved ones and allies and a love for his nation.7]
