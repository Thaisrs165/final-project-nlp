Personality
Ikki's more impatient nature often conflicted with Jinora's calm and serious demeanor and Meelo's authoritarian attitude, leading to verbal conflicts. Despite this, she was quick to make friends with others and was willing to help those in need whenever she could.2]
During Mako, Bolin, and Asami's stay at the island, Ikki seemed to keep up well with Bolin's fast-paced and testy questions. She also demonstrated an impressive level of knowledge about Air Temple Island, even being able to cite how many trees there were on the island after only a moment's thought.3]
Despite her energetic personality, she could also take the time to reflect about her surroundings, concluding to insightful wisdoms about family love.4]
