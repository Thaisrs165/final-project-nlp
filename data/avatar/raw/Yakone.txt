Personality
After escaping prison and Republic City, Yakone began a new life in the Northern Water Tribe, where he married a local woman and fathered two sons. At first, Yakone appeared to have moved on from his past, finding a new happiness with his family. However, upon realizing that his children were waterbenders, he began training them in the art of bloodbending, hoping that they would eventually be skilled enough to avenge him by killing the Avatar. Allowing his hatred and quest for vengeance to consume him, he relentlessly drilled his sons, slowly beginning to view them as tools for revenge instead of people. He did not tolerate any opposition and had no qualms about openly showing admiration for Noatak's abilities while demeaning Tarrlok's. His thirst for revenge even led him to force the boys to bloodbend animals and eventually each other. When Tarrlok refused to bloodbend his brother and was about to receive punishment from his father, Yakone was bloodbent by Noatak and called the true weakling.
After Noatak ran away, Yakone's personality changed dramatically. Heartbroken at the prospect of having caused his son's death and never being able to get back at Aang, he stopped training Tarrlok and was no longer focused on getting revenge on the Avatar, withering away and eventually passing away a few years later.2]
