Who is Izumi in the "Avatar" universe?
Izumi is a character in the "Avatar" universe, specifically in "The Legend of Korra" series. She is the daughter of Zuko, and the current Fire Lord of that time. She ascended the throne in 167 AG following her father's abdication.
Provided by: Community
Who are the parents of Fire Lord Izumi?
Fire Lord Izumi is the daughter of Fire Lord Zuko. Her mother's identity is not mentioned in the series.
Provided by: Fandom
Who is Izumi's son?
General Iroh is Fire Lord Izumi's son.
Provided by: Community
What is the lineage of Fire Lord Izumi?
Fire Lord Izumi is the daughter of Fire Lord Zuko, making her the granddaughter of Ozai and Ursa. She is also the niece of Azula and Kiyi. Izumi has two children, a son named Iroh, named after her great-uncle, and a daughter.
Provided by: Community
Who ascended the throne of the Fire Nation after Lord Zuko?
Izumi, the daughter of Lord Zuko, ascended the throne of the Fire Nation after him. She is notable as the first woman to ascend to the throne in generations.
