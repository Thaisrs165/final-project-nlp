Quick Answers
Who were the family members of Fire Lord Ozai?
Fire Lord Ozai, also known as Phoenix King Ozai, was a member of the Fire Nation Royal Family. His father was Fire Lord Azulon, a powerful firebender trained by his own father, Fire Lord Sozin. Ozai had a brother named Iroh. Ozai's son is Zuko, who succeeded him as the Fire Lord. The family tree also includes historical figures like Yosor and Gonryu, who are presumably ancestors, and other descendants of the Fire Nation Royal Family.
Provided by: Community
What was Ozai's reign period as the Fire Lord?
Ozai, also known as Phoenix King Ozai, was the ruler of the Fire Nation during the final years of the Hundred Year War. His reign as the Fire Lord lasted from 95 to 100 AG.
Provided by: Fandom
Why is Ozai also known as the Phoenix King?
Ozai crowned himself as the Phoenix King just before the arrival of Sozin's Comet. This title was created by Ozai himself to signify his supreme rule over the entire world once the Fire Nation had won the Hundred Year War. The title also allowed his daughter, Azula, to become the Fire Lord and lead the Fire Nation in his absence. However, Ozai only held the title of Phoenix King for two days, as the position was abolished after his defeat.
Provided by: Community
How did Ozai's rule impact the Hundred Year War?
Fire Lord Ozai's rule had a significant impact on the Hundred Year War. His reign saw significant technological advancements and the fall of the Earth Kingdom. However, it was also during his rule that Avatar Aang reemerged. Despite Ozai's attempts to capture and defeat the Avatar, he was unsuccessful. The Avatar's intervention eventually led to the Fire Nation's defeat and Ozai's deposition in favor of his son, Zuko, marking the end of the Hundred Year War. Ozai's rule was characterized by his aggressive pursuit of power, which ultimately led to his downfall.
Provided by: Fandom
What is the relationship between Ozai and his son, Zuko?
Ozai and Zuko had a complex father-son relationship. Ozai was never pleased with Zuko, despite Zuko's best efforts, and said that he was lucky to be born. Ozai had disdain for Zuko's moralistic nature, an attitude that worsened after Ursa stated her desire that Zuko turn out nothing like Ozai. When Azulon ordered Ozai to kill Zuko, Ozai was willing to do as his father had commanded, an act that was only prevented at Ursa's intervention. After Zuko spoke out during a War Room meeting, Ozai burned Zuko's eye during an Agni Kai, permanently scarring his son. Ozai then banished Zuko, stating he could only return home if he captured the Avatar.
Provided by: Community
Ozai was easily angered, domineering, and insatiably power-hungry. He exhibited these traits from an early age, and Iroh noted that they stemmed from a deep-seated pathological psychology. According to him, Ozai lacked empathy,2] was "not the understanding type",3] and never regretted, forgave, or changed his mind about anything.4] Believing morality to be a sign of weakness, Ozai found peace and kindness laughable concepts and despised those who followed moral principles. As such, he possessed little to no empathy and rarely showed any interest in the feelings of others.5]6]7]
This behavior was strongly influenced by his obsession with control, and in order to achieve total control over the Fire Nation, Ozai was even willing to sacrifice his own family. Confronting Ursa about a false letter she wrote claiming Zuko was Ikem's son after she suspected Ozai had intercepted all her letters home, she declared her hope that their son would never turn out like Ozai. Ozai in turn proclaimed that onward he would treat Zuko cruelly as if he was not his son, all just to spite Ursa.8] When ordered by Azulon to kill Zuko so that he would know the pain of losing a son,9] Ozai admitted to Zuko that he would have gladly carried out the murder had Ursa not interfered.6]
Eventually, he murdered his own father in order to usurp his brother's birthright and become Fire Lord.10] Similarly, he did not hesitate to sacrifice his subordinates if that meant he would obtain more power. As a result, Ozai supported the idea of sacrificing new recruits to defeat a powerful Earth Kingdom battalion so he could get closer to controlling Ba Sing Se.11] Likewise, he decided to use the power of Sozin's Comet to destroy the Earth Kingdom without standing still with the human loss that would mean.12] Despite that, Ozai did believe that a Fire Lord's duty was to protect his people, though he reasoned that was because they were extensions of his divine will as Fire Lord; by attacking them, an opponent attacked him.5] The ultimate expression of his obsession with control was his aspiration in life to conquer the entire world.12]
Nevertheless, in his younger years, Ozai showed limited care and compassion for his own family. Despite their strained relationship, Ozai and Iroh were closer while they were still in school, though the latter distanced himself from his brother when he realized that he had a "heart of ice".2] When Ursa begged him to spare her former lover, Ozai complied to the request after being called "My Love" by his future wife.13] When he planned to cast his infant son from the palace, Ozai gave in after Ursa and the Fire Sages pleaded with him to give Zuko a chance.10] Ozai believed that he attempted to be a good father in his early years,5] a view shared by both Zuko and Azula.13] However, his pride, anger, and lust for power degraded his relationship with his family. He believed skill and accomplishment determined worth, which allowed the naturally cunning and talented Azula to rise to the position of his favorite child, while he ignored or psychologically abused Zuko. He had no qualms about making Zuko's life miserable just to get back at Ursa for snapping at him that she wished that Zuko was Ikem's son, rather than his.8]
While his relationship with Zuko had always been tense, thenceforth Ozai began to torture Zuko with his perceived inferiority, saying that Azula was "born lucky", while he was "lucky to be born".14] After his wife's banishment, he continued to be psychologically abusive toward both of his children and physically abusive toward his son. When Zuko was thirteen, Ozai burned his face and banished him from his country, simply because Zuko spoke out of turn during a war meeting,11] an action for which he never showed any remorse8] and merely claimed it to have been a lesson in respect.6] He only acknowledged Zuko when he helped his sister conquer Ba Sing Se and when he "killed" the Avatar.15] Eventually, Ozai attempted to kill Zuko when the latter announced his intent to join the Avatar, thereby defying his father.6] On the other hand, Ozai raised Azula to become his future heir, expecting nothing less than the best from her,9] which placed a great amount of stress on her and forced her to become a perfectionist.4] Ultimately, it was the environment in which Ozai raised Azula, where fear, power, and perfection were the only reliable truths, that set the groundwork for her total mental collapse.1]
However, despite his behavior toward his children, Ozai appeared to be remarkably forgiving of failure for certain people, such as War Minister Qin6]16]17] and Zhao, who retained their positions and, in Zhao's case, was repeatedly promoted,18] despite repeated failures and bungles. What mattered most to Ozai, however, was not necessarily their competence, but their total loyalty to his cause. In contrast, he had no tolerance for those who voiced opposition to his ideas, executing or exiling several advisors who dared to oppose some of his decrees.19] His patience was also limited for those who failed to impress him such as Kunyo or Vachir.10] In general, Ozai saw his subordinates as expendable and replaceable.19]
By the time Ozai crowned himself Phoenix King, he began to exhibit extreme megalomania, claiming to own the Earth and to "have all the power in the world".1]
―Ozai questions Zuko about the challenges of being Fire Lord.20]
After being stripped of both his bending and his throne, Ozai apparently still retained his domineering personality, as he casually lectured Fire Lord Zuko when he came to visit Ozai in jail. However, he would develop a calmer, more logical attitude toward life as he thought that Aang's belief of maintaining the four separate nations would only halt progress. This is apparently true as Yu Dao, a city that combined the effort from citizens of both the Fire Nation and the Earth Kingdom, grew from a small village to a very developed area. As Zuko sought out Ozai for advice, through their discussions, it was revealed that Ozai believed that good and evil, and even strength and weakness, were meaningless concepts. He believed that the only right and wrong were what he, or more accurately, the Fire Lord, decided, due to his divine right to rule. Any choice he made was the right one, simply by virtue of it being his choice, and that indecisiveness and attempts to guide oneself by moral principles were signs of weakness, traits he despised in Zuko.5]
