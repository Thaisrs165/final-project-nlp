# The Unsure Hat

Answer 12 questions once. A frozen BERT, Sentence-BERT and an NLI model then sort you into
five worlds (Harry Potter, Divergent, Game of Thrones, Avatar and a world we made up) and show
their answers side by side. A second tab tricks BERT with names and "not"; a third compares the
models with real people on vague descriptions.

Part of the NLP final project "Does BERT actually sort you?" (Insper, 2026).
Code and report: https://github.com/Thaisrs165/final-project-nlp
